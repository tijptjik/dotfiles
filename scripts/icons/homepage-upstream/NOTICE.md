# Homepage logo references

Downloaded from `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons/` on
2026-10-27. The accompanying Apache-2.0 LICENSE is from that collection.
Application names, marks and other third-party rights remain with their owners.
The themed SVG adaptations are unofficial. `homepage.json` records source URLs
and reference hashes. Original reference images and visual comparisons are archived locally under
`.local/icon-reviews/` and are not build inputs or committed assets.

The references record the previous live dashboard. Grimmory previously used
Booklore's mark. Profilarr's PNG has circular arrows; its collection SVG instead
has sliders. Shelfmark's collection SVG was unavailable. The current themed
Grimmory, Profilarr, Shelfmark, Seerr, Audiobookshelf, Radarr and Sonarr artwork
uses original illustrative concepts requested by the user, rather than tracing
these historical logos. See `homepage.json` design notes.
