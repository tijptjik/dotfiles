# ArtCraft identity references

The fifteen Craft apps listed at <https://getartcraft.com/apps> were checked on
2026-11-03. Reference URLs and image hashes are recorded per app in
`scripts/icons/provenance.json`. Unmodified website images are local review
artifacts, not build inputs or release payloads.

The Rose Punk artwork uses new, hand-authored vector paths in
`scripts/icons/material_art.py`, with editable snapshots in `local/`. The
drawings retain the subjects and distinguishing features of the app identities;
they do not trace, embed or recolour the upstream engravings. App names and
trademark rights remain with the ArtCraft Team and respective owners. This is
an unofficial theme, not an ArtCraft product or endorsement.

The fourteen permissively licensed reference identities retain their upstream
MIT notices here. Icon-specific license declarations or attribution files are
included where available. PhotoCraft, VectorCraft, FilmCraft, LightCraft,
PdfCraft, EffectCraft, DesignCraft, FigCraft and TextureCraft credit original
drawings made by their project owners. The other five describe original SVG
object or bird artwork by their contributors.

Craft Anything's upstream raven is explicitly excluded from its software
license. Its notice is retained; the new Rose Punk raven is an independent
drawing, with different contour and feather geometry, not an adaptation of the
restricted vector paths. No MIT license is asserted over the upstream raven.

Studio and Launcher use the restricted ArtCraft A mark. Their logo is left
unchanged and is not vendored or added to this theme. The launcher's brand
license is retained as evidence of that scope decision.
